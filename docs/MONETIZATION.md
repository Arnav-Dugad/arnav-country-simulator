# Monetization study and demo store

*Prepared 2026-11-07. Prices are snapshots from public store listings and change over time.*

## 1. What the genre sells

| Game | Model | What they sell (examples) |
|---|---|---|
| **Dictator 2: Evolution / Political Game** | Free + IAP | Premium currency "Tyrants" packs (700 for $2.99–$4.99, up to 8000 for $99.99), a scenario add-on ($0.99), card bundles, remove ads ($4.99). Reviews complain about forced ads and a pay-to-win feel. |
| **Dictator – Rule the World** (Playgendary) | Free + IAP + subscriptions | Weekly $7.99, monthly $19.99, yearly $99.99 subscriptions; remove ads $4.99; "Golden Tyrants" packs $1.99–$49.99. |
| **Dictator: Emergence** | Free + IAP | Remove ads $0.99; currency packs up to $99.99. |
| **Pocket Politics / Pocket Politics 2** | Free + IAP + VIP | Gold packs $4.99–$19.99, "The Steal Deal" starter bundle $5.99, Premium Rewards $4.99, VIP subscription $2.99/week or $7.99/month. |
| **Democracy 4** (PC/console, premium) | Paid + DLC | Base game ~$29.99; Country Pack $9.99, Event Pack $6.99, Voting Systems $4.99; all-in bundle. |

**The patterns:**
1. **Premium currency** used to blur real prices.
2. **Remove-ads**, after first adding intrusive ads.
3. **Weekly and monthly VIP subscriptions** at high prices.
4. **Starter bundles**.
5. **Content add-ons** (scenarios, events, countries).
6. **Whale packs** up to $99.99.

## 2. Our approach for the demo store

What we **use**, because it gives players fair value:
- **Content packs** (the Democracy 4 model): scenarios, event packs and reform packs, each with a clear real-money price and a list of exactly what's included.
- **Cosmetics** that genuinely change the look: map themes, app icon variants and leader badges.
- **Supporter tips** with a thank-you cosmetic.
- **Statesman Pass**, an optional subscription. It includes a monthly cosmetic theme, early access to new scenarios and extra cloud save slots. It never gates core gameplay or understanding.
- **Complete Edition:** a one-time bundle of everything, priced below the sum of its parts.

What we **don't** use, and why:
- **Premium currency.** It hides what things really cost. We show real prices only.
- **Ads and remove-ads.** Ads break the calm, premium feel, and we have none.
- **Pay-to-win boosts** (buying approval, treasury or political capital). They break the simulation's honesty.
- **Loot boxes and random rewards.** They're gambling-like, and illegal or restricted in some countries.
- **Countdown timers and FOMO bundles.** These are pressure tactics.
- **$7.99/week subscriptions or $99.99 whale packs.** Predatory pricing.
