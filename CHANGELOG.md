# Changelog

The release workflow publishes the section that matches the tag as the GitHub Release notes.

## [0.11.0] - 2026-11-07

**Challenges**
- Seven challenges with a real country, a clear goal and a deadline: tame Argentina's inflation, grow India to $10 trillion, bring jobs to South Africa, balance Italy's budget, get Greece out of debt, win three terms in Japan, calm Nigeria
- Your progress shows at the top of Nation health; finishing one earns the new "Challenger" achievement
- Find them under Challenges in the country picker

**Smarter economy**
- Unemployment now responds properly to good policy: education lowers it over time, and long stretches of low unemployment become the new normal

## [0.10.0] - 2026-11-07

- **Share your legacy**: share your end-of-term grade, or any Year in Review, as an image through Android's share sheet
- **Country picker**: sort by name, biggest economy, richest per person or hardest start, and a "Surprise me" button

## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-07

**A world that lives**
- Other countries hold their own elections. New leaders can warm to you or turn cold
- World news: recessions, booms, defaults and new leaders among your partners, neighbours and the big economies, in the Events tab with flags (big stories pop up)

**Achievements**
- 22 achievements to earn across all your games, from "Lawmaker" to "Dynasty" and "Triple A"
- Each must be earned by improving on where your country started: no free trophies
- Find them in Settings > Achievements

## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-07

- **Your legacy**: when your term ends you get a grade from A+ to F, with your strongest and weakest areas
- **Scrub the charts**: drag across any history chart to see the exact value for any month
- Year-on-year changes that round to zero now say "No change"

## [0.7.0] - 2026-11-07

**For everyone**
- New Accessibility settings: text size (Small to Larger), a colour-blind friendly map (blue and orange instead of red and green), and Reduce transparency (solid panels: easier to read and faster on older phones)
- Layouts adapt to larger text: labels shorten gracefully instead of overflowing

**Easier to play**
- A new home screen: continue your last game in one tap, see your other saves, or lead a new nation
- "Undo my changes" in Economy and Budget, so you can experiment freely
- "Save and go to the main menu" in Settings

## [0.6.0] - 2026-11-07

- **Credit rating** (AAA to C) in Economy: tap it to see exactly what goes into it. Starting ratings closely match real ones. Headlines when you're upgraded or downgraded
- **Budget tile** shows your surplus or deficit at a glance
- **World rank**: see where you stand in GDP, wealth per person, happiness and stability
- The World tab is clearer, with a friendlier summary
- Saves are now written safely in the background, so cloud sync never reads a half-written save

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-07

**Elections you can feel**
- Election campaigns: three months before each vote, pick two themes to run on. Each theme shows your record; campaign on your strengths, because a weak record can backfire
- Election night: watch the votes count up live against the 50% line before the result lands

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-07

**Events 2.0**
- 20 new events, for 36 in total: monsoon floods, hurricanes, heatwaves, earthquakes and droughts where they really happen
- Oil-rich countries (using real export data) face oil windfalls and price crashes
- Story chains: a pandemic leads to a vaccine race, a scandal inquiry reports back, recessions end in recovery booms
- Every choice now shows its effects up front (costs in real money, growth, approval and more)
- Housing crises, automation, brain drain, tourism booms and the chance to host the world games

**Smoother**
- No more stutter at the start of each month or during the yearly autosave
- Settings opens instantly, even with several long games saved

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-07

**Laws & reforms**
- New Laws tab with 16 reforms across health, economy, environment and governance: universal healthcare, free university, carbon tax, minimum wage, wealth tax, pension age and more
- Laws take months to pass (or repeal); each shows its effects and yearly cost before you commit
- Countries start with the laws they really have, so changes are what count

**A living story**
- Headlines when big things happen: GDP milestones, record-low inflation, winning back a majority
- A Year in Review card every January
- Capital cities on the map (your capital in gold), and labels never hide behind the controls

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-07

**Easier to understand**
- Tap any number in Economy to see *why* it's moving: every factor that pushes it, in plain words, plus tips
- Live forecast: as you move a slider, see what happens in 12 months (growth, prices, jobs, approval, budget)
- Nation health card: Thriving / Doing okay / Struggling, with the three most useful things to do next
- A guided tour on your first game (replay any time from Settings)

**Feels better**
- Original sound design: soft taps, sheet whooshes, event chimes, election swells, and a calm ambient soundtrack (toggles in Settings)
- The map now has soft, springy edges, pinch-zoom momentum, and long-press on a country to open it
- The speed control now shows Pause correctly

## [0.1.2] - 2026-11-07

- Real country flags across the game
- New players are invited to create a free account (or play as a backed-up guest)
- Demo store in Settings: map themes you can try (Gold Leaf, Aurora, Graphite, Ember), expansions,
  Statesman Pass and supporter items. Purchases are simulated and nothing is charged
- Calmer top bar and a cleaner glow around your country

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-07

- Cloud saves are live: play as a guest with automatic backup, or add an email to use your saves on any device
- Saves sync in the background and catch up after you've been offline
- Clearer text over glass panels, smoother sheets, sharper country outlines
- Fairer start in tough countries (new-leader honeymoon)

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-07

First release.

- Lead any of 185 real countries, starting from real 2025 World Bank and IMF data
- Interactive world map with pinch-zoom, smooth panning and tap-to-select, plus five map modes
- Monthly economy with taxes, interest rate, trade policy and a six-part budget
- Diplomacy: trade deals, alliances, foreign aid and sanctions
- 16 random events with real tradeoffs, plus elections every four years
- Three save slots with yearly autosave; optional cloud saves (guest or email)
- In-app updates from GitHub Releases
