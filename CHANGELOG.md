# Changelog

The release workflow publishes the section that matches the tag as the GitHub Release notes.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-07

**Easier to understand**
- Tap any number in Economy to see *why* it's moving: every factor that pushes it, in plain words, plus tips
- Live forecast: as you move a slider, see what happens in 12 months (growth, prices, jobs, approval, budget)
- Nation health card: Thriving / Doing okay / Struggling, with the three most useful things to do next
- A guided tour on your first game (replay any time from Settings)

**Feels better**
- Original sound design: soft taps, sheet whooshes, event chimes, election swells, and a calm ambient soundtrack (toggles in Settings)
- The map now has soft, springy edges, pinch-zoom momentum, and long-press on a country to open it
- The speed control now shows Pause correctly

## [0.1.2] - 2026-11-07

- Real country flags across the game
- New players are invited to create a free account (or play as a backed-up guest)
- Demo store in Settings: map themes you can try (Gold Leaf, Aurora, Graphite, Ember), expansions,
  Statesman Pass and supporter items. Purchases are simulated and nothing is charged
- Calmer top bar and a cleaner glow around your country

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-07

- Cloud saves are live: play as a guest with automatic backup, or add an email to use your saves on any device
- Saves sync in the background and catch up after you've been offline
- Clearer text over glass panels, smoother sheets, sharper country outlines
- Fairer start in tough countries (new-leader honeymoon)

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-07

First release.

- Lead any of 185 real countries, starting from real 2025 World Bank and IMF data
- Interactive world map with pinch-zoom, smooth panning and tap-to-select, plus five map modes
- Monthly economy with taxes, interest rate, trade policy and a six-part budget
- Diplomacy: trade deals, alliances, foreign aid and sanctions
- 16 random events with real tradeoffs, plus elections every four years
- Three save slots with yearly autosave; optional cloud saves (guest or email)
- In-app updates from GitHub Releases
