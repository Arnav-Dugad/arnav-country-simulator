# Changelog

The release workflow publishes the section that matches the tag as the GitHub Release notes.

## [0.46.0] - 2026-11-08

- Balance: a successor candidate now carries some of the party's fatigue after long years in power, so term limits don't make late elections easier (the balance report showed Realistic had become too forgiving)
- Calmer Inbox: foreign leaders ask for disaster aid at most once every 8 months
- Fewer repeats: the same event no longer comes back within two and a half years, and a new world games bid can't arrive while you're still hosting one
- **The resource curse:** after a big mineral discovery, the mining money arrives 18 months later. Save it in a national wealth fund (like Norway), spend it on people now (popular, but prices rise and factories suffer), or pay down the debt

## [0.45.2] - 2026-11-08

- An original fanfare plays when you win an election and when the world games open
- Balance: TV debates swing fewer votes (the balance report showed elections on Realistic had become too easy), and defending a weak record now really hurts

## [0.45.1] - 2026-11-08

- "Your voters" shows today's race: "If the vote were today: you 54%, Lucas Almeida 46%", with the opposition leader's face

## [0.45.0] - 2026-11-08

- Country pages and the country picker show term limits ("2-term limit", "one term only")
- The What's new card closes with a tap anywhere outside it

## [0.40.0] - 2026-11-08

- **Migration.** Every country starts at its real net migration (UN data). Rising incomes and happiness draw people in; unrest, joblessness and falling living standards push them out
- Newcomers grow the population and the workforce, giving extra growth (most of all in ageing countries) and a little more competition for jobs at first. Both show up in the "Why?" explainers
- New law: **Skilled-worker visas** (already in force in countries like Canada, Australia and Germany). Tougher border controls now also cut arrivals
- Country pages show migration, with headlines when the flow turns around
- The referendum button now reads "Or hold a referendum" (no longer cut off on small phones)
- Faster monthly simulation (climate lookups are cached)

## [0.39.0] - 2026-11-08

- They go after your weakest record in the Inbox ("Prices are out of control…"), you meet them on the campaign card, and election night shows you side by side
- Beat them and the opposition picks a new leader to take you on

## [0.38.0] - 2026-11-08

- **Term limits.** Presidents now face their country's real limit: two terms in the United States, Brazil, France, Kenya, Indonesia and more; a single term in Mexico, South Korea, the Philippines, Colombia and others. A year before your last election you choose:
  - **Step down with honour:** your game ends at the election with a legacy bonus (and the "Peaceful handover" achievement)
  - **Back a successor:** a new candidate from your party runs; win and you keep playing
  - **Change the constitution:** a gamble that depends on your approval. Democracies object, and if the courts block it your party must find a successor anyway
- The top bar shows "You step down 2034" or "Successor vote 2034" when it applies

## [0.37.1] - 2026-11-08

- Headlines when the world passes 1.5 °C, 1.75 °C and 2 °C of warming

## [0.37.0] - 2026-11-08

- **Climate.** Every country starts at its real CO₂ emissions per person and renewable energy share (World Bank). Emissions follow your economy and fall with solar, wind, nuclear, hydro, a coal phase-out, a carbon tax, electric-car subsidies, rail and new forests (cards now show "CO₂ −7%")
- **A warming world:** the world's total emissions set how fast the planet warms (starting from today's real +1.4 °C). A warmer world brings more floods, storms, heatwaves and droughts, at home and abroad
- **COP climate conferences** every other November: pledge a bold cut, a modest one, or nothing. Six years later you're judged. Keep it and partners cheer; miss it and they remember
- New **Climate** card on the World page, an **Emissions** map layer, CO₂ in Compare and on every country page, a "Cut emissions" goal for big emitters, and the "Promise kept" achievement
- New AI-generated scene for climate events

**Calm, not naggy**

**Smarter, steadier economy**
- An independent central bank now sets interest rates for you (switch it off in Economy to do it yourself)

**Also new**

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-07

**Laws & reforms**
- New Laws tab with 16 reforms across health, economy, environment and governance: universal healthcare, free university, carbon tax, minimum wage, wealth tax, pension age and more
- Laws take months to pass (or repeal); each shows its effects and yearly cost before you commit
- Countries start with the laws they really have, so changes are what count

**A living story**
- Headlines when big things happen: GDP milestones, record-low inflation, winning back a majority
- A Year in Review card every January
- Capital cities on the map (your capital in gold), and labels never hide behind the controls

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-07

**Easier to understand**
- Tap any number in Economy to see *why* it's moving: every factor that pushes it, in plain words, plus tips
- Live forecast: as you move a slider, see what happens in 12 months (growth, prices, jobs, approval, budget)
- Nation health card: Thriving / Doing okay / Struggling, with the three most useful things to do next
- A guided tour on your first game (replay any time from Settings)

**Feels better**
- Original sound design: soft taps, sheet whooshes, event chimes, election swells, and a calm ambient soundtrack (toggles in Settings)
- The map now has soft, springy edges, pinch-zoom momentum, and long-press on a country to open it
- The speed control now shows Pause correctly

## [0.1.2] - 2026-11-07

- Real country flags across the game
- New players are invited to create a free account (or play as a backed-up guest)
- Demo store in Settings: map themes you can try (Gold Leaf, Aurora, Graphite, Ember), expansions,
  Statesman Pass and supporter items. Purchases are simulated and nothing is charged
- Calmer top bar and a cleaner glow around your country

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-07

- Cloud saves are live: play as a guest with automatic backup, or add an email to use your saves on any device
- Saves sync in the background and catch up after you've been offline
- Clearer text over glass panels, smoother sheets, sharper country outlines
- Fairer start in tough countries (new-leader honeymoon)

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-07

First release.

- Lead any of 185 real countries, starting from real 2025 World Bank and IMF data
- Interactive world map with pinch-zoom, smooth panning and tap-to-select, plus five map modes
- Monthly economy with taxes, interest rate, trade policy and a six-part budget
- Diplomacy: trade deals, alliances, foreign aid and sanctions
- 16 random events with real tradeoffs, plus elections every four years
- Three save slots with yearly autosave; optional cloud saves (guest or email)
- In-app updates from GitHub Releases
