# Changelog

The release workflow publishes the section that matches the tag as the GitHub Release notes.

## [0.19.0] - 2026-11-08

**Who supports you, and why**
- Voter groups: Workers, Business, Young people, Retirees and Rural voters, sized from your country's real population (how old, how urban)
- A new "Your voters" card in Economy shows each group's support and the biggest reason behind it ("Angry about: prices", "Likes: free university")
- Every law now says who would cheer and who would object
- Election night shows how each group voted

## [0.18.0] - 2026-11-08

**National goals**
- Three goals picked from your own country's weak spots (tame prices, more jobs, cut the debt, a calmer country, raise incomes, balance the budget…), always measured from where you started
- Progress rings in Economy; reaching a goal brings a burst of gold, a little approval and a new goal. Goals reached count toward your legacy grade

**Fixed**
- Two elections close together while fast-forwarding no longer leave the vote count animation running on a closed card

## [0.17.1] - 2026-11-08

- **Fixed: researched country details were missing on phones.** Taglines, main exports, trading partners, friends and rivals, natural risks, strengths and challenges now appear on every country card on Android too (and shape the game as intended)

## [0.17.0] - 2026-11-08

**Face the press**
- Press conferences: a few times a year reporters ask about the weakest part of your record (prices, jobs, debt, growth, unrest), using your real numbers. Be honest, spin it or deflect
- Your credibility with the press now matters: spin works better when people trust you, and when they don't, bad news hits harder

**Understand your year**
- New "Over the last year" card in Economy: every key number's change with a sparkline and what's lifting it or holding it back

**Fairer for fragile countries** (found by playing Mali)
- Military governments no longer face restless generals in their very first month: the army now judges you against where your country started
- The same for unrest in countries without elections

**More believable people**
- Leaders and ministers now have names that fit their own country (German names in Germany, Japanese in Japan, Brazilian in Brazil…), across 30 countries and every region
- Food price headlines get a farming icon

## [0.16.0] - 2026-11-07

**A world full of people**
- **Every country has a leader**: an original, fictional president, prime minister or monarch with a portrait and a personality (Trader, Nationalist, Hawk, Peacemaker, Reformer, Pragmatist). Personalities change how they react to your deals and alliances, and drift relations over time. New leaders take over after elections and upheavals, and the news names them
- **Call another leader** from their country card: talk about shared history, press them on a dispute, or ask them to invest. How it lands depends on who they are
- **Your cabinet has faces**: portraits for your four ministers, distinct for each post
- **Your portrait**: pick one of 14 portraits (or keep your initials) in Settings > Your profile

**Painted scenes**
- 14 cinematic scene paintings (summits, protests, floods, hurricanes, markets, parliament, hospitals, oil, drought, tech, celebrations, ports, construction, election night) on event cards, elections, the year in review, advisor tips and the Build, Laws and World pages, with a slow, gentle camera drift

**Elections**
- Opinion polls: in the two years before an election, the top bar shows how you'd do if the vote were today

All portraits and scenes are AI-generated art of original, fictional people and places. No real people are depicted.

- Landscape: side panels use the full height, and turning your phone brings your country back into view

## [0.15.1] - 2026-11-07

## [0.15.0] - 2026-11-07

**A world that works together**
- **World markets**: oil and food prices rise and fall. Oil exporters gain when prices jump, importers pay more, and food spikes hit poorer countries hardest. See them on the World page
- **Booms and busts spread**: when the world economy or your region slows down, open economies feel it, and your trade partners pass on their good and bad times
- **Treaties & blocs**: the Paris climate agreement, the EU, NATO, ASEAN, Mercosur, the African Free Trade Area, USMCA, the Gulf council, BRICS and more, with their real members. Apply to join or leave; members react. Blocs grow over time
- **UN votes and summits**: each September a UN vote, each November a summit. How you vote warms or cools whole camps of countries

**Your government**
- **A cabinet** of four ministers with traits (Technocrat, Reformer, Diplomat, Builder, Hawk…). Reshuffle them; their advice now comes in their name
- **Your profile**: add your name, party and colour. They appear in headlines, on election night and on your legacy card

**Every country, deeper** (researched for 125 countries)
- A tagline, main exports, trading partners, friends and rivals, natural risks, strengths and challenges, plus one key fact on every country card
- Strengths and challenges change how things work: tech hubs get more from research, red tape slows laws and building, stable institutions soften shocks, and events follow each country's real risks

**See more, understand more**
- **Compare** any country with yours, side by side
- Charts: pick 1 year, 5 years or all; see your decisions as dots on the line; and a dashed 12-month forecast
- **Illustrations**: calm animated scenes on events, decisions, elections, tips and page headers
- **Adaptive music** that turns tense when your country is in trouble and calm when it thrives

**Easier to use**
- A clean new Settings: your profile on top, then short groups that open their own pages
- **Landscape mode**: turn your phone sideways for a wide map with panels on the side (Settings > Display)
- **Sandbox**: start any country from any situation (growth, inflation, debt, approval, system of government, no random events)
- **Save conflicts**: if this phone and the cloud both changed a save, you choose which to keep (or keep both)

## [0.14.1] - 2026-11-07

**A living map**
- **Globe view**: pinch out past the whole world (or tap Layers > Globe view) and the map curls into a globe that turns to face your country and spins slowly. Drag to spin it, tap a country, pinch in, double-tap or tap Flat map to come back
- **Trade flows**: glowing arcs from your partners to you, thicker for bigger economies, with light travelling along them (gold for allies)
- **Calm weather**: a slow swirl over a hurricane, a warm haze in a heatwave, rain over floods, ripples after an earthquake. Disasters now also strike other countries in the news
- Your construction projects appear as small pins around your capital, with progress rings
- A ripple where you tap, and a pill that slides behind the active tab

**Fairer, more varied games** (tuned with 600 simulated games)
- Voters tire of long-serving governments: each term you win makes the next election a little harder
- Relaxed, Standard and Realistic now feel clearly different
- Fragile countries no longer collapse just because they started unstable
- Slightly fewer global recessions

**Faster and smoother**
- Faster start: the map is prepared ahead of time and the first screen loads while the logo animates
- Panels fade in section by section
- Games saved before construction existed now get their countries' real starting infrastructure

## [0.13.0] - 2026-11-07

**Calm, not naggy**
- No more pop-ups every few months. Events now wait in a small pill above the dock with a countdown ring: answer when you're ready, or your cabinet picks the recommended option after six months. Only truly critical moments (elections, a debt crisis) pause the game
- A new **Inbox** (the bell): decisions, headlines, finished projects, world news and your history, all in one calm place
- The year in review and election campaigns no longer interrupt you either
- Banners show one at a time, spaced out. Choose **Calm** or **Everything**, and how events work (Inbox, Ask me, or let the Cabinet decide) in Settings > Notifications

**Much easier to use**
- New layout, based on research into how great strategy and map apps work: play/pause and speed sit right under your thumb, the "what needs you next" pill is always one tap away, and map views moved into a Layers button (with a legend)
- Five clear tabs: Economy, Budget, **Build**, Laws, World. Inbox and Settings are in the top bar
- Tap the date to pause, tap your flag to see your country, tap the map to close the country card
- Fixed: the close (X) button on panels didn't work
- Advisor tips: when something needs fixing, the pill tells you what and takes you there

**Every country is different**
- Real systems of government: presidential and parliamentary democracies, constitutional and absolute monarchies, one-party states, military governments, theocracies. Each with its real leader title and term length (India 5 years, Mexico 6, Australia 3…)
- No elections where there are none: one-party states face a Party Congress, military rulers must keep the generals happy, and anywhere unrest can bring you down. Dominant-party states hold elections tilted their way
- A reform to bring free elections to closed systems
- 14 new events shaped by your country: coalition crises, budget standoffs, court rulings, dissidents, succession questions, the generals' demands, pension crunches, blackouts, young job-seekers and more
- Real starting budgets (health, education, research), trade, inequality, ageing and infrastructure from World Bank data, plus traits like "Oil & gas exporter", "Ageing population" or "Landlocked"

**Build your country**
- 23 construction projects: highways, rural roads, high-speed rail, metros, ports, airports, power grids, solar, wind, nuclear, dams, hospitals, schools, universities, housing, clean water, broadband, tech parks, industry, tourism, a space programme, flood defences and forests
- What you can build depends on your country (no ports when landlocked, power grids where homes still lack electricity…). Projects employ people while built and pay off for decades. A bigger infrastructure budget builds faster; corruption makes them cost more

**Smarter, steadier economy**
- An independent central bank now sets interest rates for you (switch it off in Economy to do it yourself)
- Welfare matters more in unequal countries; ageing slows growth, young populations speed it up
- Choose a difficulty when you start: Relaxed, Standard or Realistic

**Also new**
- Cheats in Settings: money, debt wipe, instant construction, never losing power and more (cheated games don't earn achievements)
- Delete your account (and all cloud saves) from Settings
- Map: double-tap zooms in and two-finger tap zooms out, with haptic ticks; long camera moves glide in a gentle arc
- Election night: the world dims while your country breathes; a win ends in a gold sweep, a loss fades to grey
- Four new achievements
