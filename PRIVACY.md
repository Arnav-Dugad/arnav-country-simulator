# Privacy policy

_Last updated 8 November 2026_

Arnav Country Simulator is a game. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains the little data it does handle, and why.

## What stays on your phone

Your games, settings, achievements and career records are saved on your device. They never leave it unless you use cloud backup (below).

## Your account and cloud backup

To back up your games, the app signs you in with Google Firebase Authentication: either anonymously (a random ID, no personal details) or with the email address and password you choose. Your saved games are then stored in Google Cloud Firestore, where only your account can read or change them.

- **Stored:** your account ID, your email address (only if you sign up with one), your saved games and the date each was saved.
- **Not stored:** your name, contacts, location, device identifiers or anything else on your phone.

## The weekly leaderboard

If you finish a weekly challenge, your best score is posted to a public leaderboard together with the leader name you chose in the game (or "A leader" if you didn't set one), the country you played and your grade. Anyone using the app can see the top scores. Don't use your real full name as your leader name if you'd rather not be identified.

## Updates

The app checks GitHub for new versions. That request contains no personal information.

## Deleting your data

In the app, open **Settings › Account & cloud › Delete account**. This permanently deletes your cloud saves and your account. Saves on your phone are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Playing without signing up for an email account keeps everything anonymous.

## Services we use

- Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- GitHub (app downloads and update checks): [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
