# Data sources

All bundled data is generated by scripts in `tools/` and comes from open, freely
licensed sources. Regenerate with:

```bash
python tools/fetch_map.py            # Natural Earth -> tools/.cache/map_projected.json
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/bake_map.gd   # -> data/world_map.res
python tools/build_country_data.py   # World Bank + IMF -> data/countries.json
```

## Map geometry — `data/world_map.res`

Borders follow **India's official point of view** (Natural Earth v5.1.2 1:10m `admin_0_countries_ind`
worldview, simplified by tools/bake_map.gd): all of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh (including
Gilgit-Baltistan and Aksai Chin) are part of India. In this worldview Kosovo, Somaliland, Northern
Cyprus and Western Sahara are not shown as separate countries.
- **Natural Earth 1:50m Cultural Vectors, Admin 0 – Countries**, release **v5.1.2**
  (`ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson` from github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector).
  Public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- Projected with **Equal Earth** (Šavrič, Patterson & Jenny, 2018), simplified with
  Douglas–Peucker (tolerance 0.85 projected units), holes dropped (enclaves draw on top).

The detail fields above (health … voice) shape budgets, projects, events and traits. Missing values use
regional medians and are listed per country in `estimated_detail`.

Art: portraits (`assets/portraits`) and scene paintings (`assets/scenes`) were generated with Figma AI
(gpt-image) in November 2026 from text prompts describing original, fictional people and generic places;
no real or identifiable people are depicted. Raw files live in tools/.cache/art_raw; tools/process_art.py
makes the app-sized WebP files. World leaders and ministers in the game are fictional (sim/leaders.gd,
sim/cabinet.gd).

Country profiles (`data/country_profiles.json`, 125 countries: tagline, exports, partners, friends,
rivals, natural risks, strengths, challenges, food imports, one fact) were researched in November 2026
from the CIA World Factbook, OEC, WTO / UN Comtrade summaries and World Bank country pages, with
recent geopolitics checked by web search. Rivalries and alliances reflect late 2026 and will date.

Systems of government (sim/government.gd) are hand-curated from national constitutions (term lengths),
the EIU Democracy Index 2024 and Freedom House 2025 (free vs. managed elections), and news of the
2020–2025 coups. Landlocked and island lists (autoload/world.gd) follow the UN lists.

IMF series (NGDPD, LP, NGDP_RPCH, PCPIPCH, LUR, GGR_NGDP) fill entities the World Bank
does not cover (e.g. Taiwan). Natural Earth POP_EST / GDP_MD are a last resort.

World Bank data: CC BY 4.0 (https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses).
IMF data: free to use with attribution (https://www.imf.org/en/About/copyright-and-terms).

## Estimates (clearly flagged)
Any field still missing is filled with the **median of the country's World Bank region**
(or the world median if the region has fewer than 3 data points). Every such field is
listed in that country's `estimated` array, and the game shows an "estimated" marker.
Derived game values (happiness, approval, policy rate, tax rates, military strength)
are computed in `sim/economy.gd` from these real inputs and documented there.

## Capitals
Natural Earth 1:50m populated places (v5.1.2, public domain), features of class "Admin-0 capital";
for countries with several capitals the seat of government is used.

## Starting laws (estimated)
Which reforms a country already has at the start (sim/laws.gd `starting_set`) is a simple estimate
from income level and region, not a legal database. Only *changes* from that starting set affect play.

## Playable countries
Entities of Natural Earth type *Sovereign country*, *Country* or *Disputed* that have
real (non-estimated) population data, more than 50,000 people, and are their own
sovereign. Territories and autonomous regions are shown on the map but not playable.
